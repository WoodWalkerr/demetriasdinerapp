    Opening hours

    A closing time earlier than the opening time means after midnight (9:00 AM → 2:00 AM closes at 2 AM the next day). While you're closed, customers can still order ahead for a time you're open. The POS works at any time.

    Special days: pop-up stall, events, holidays

    These replace the normal hours on that date. Add a note (like where your pop-up stall is) and customers see it on the website.

    Happy hour & promos

    Automatic discounts by time of day, on the website and the POS. Add-ons stay full price, and the discount is rounded to whole pesos. If two promos match an item, the bigger discount wins. For order-ahead, the price follows the pickup time.

    🎟️ Stamp card

    Customers collect stamps with their mobile number: online at checkout, or the cashier types it at the POS. Stamps count once an order is paid, and come back off by themselves if it's cancelled or voided. With a full card, one item on the card is free (the most expensive one, up to your limit).

    Earn stamps on

    None picked = every drink. One stamp per item (the free one doesn't earn a stamp).

    Per card, per business day (4 AM → 4 AM). 1 = one stamp a day, however many drinks they buy. Leave empty for no limit.

    Off (recommended): anyone could type someone else's number, so rewards are claimed at the counter, where the cashier can check.

    Add or remove stamps by hand

    💌 Newsletter

    Customers leave their email in a pop-up on the website (once per visit; again 14 days after "No thanks"), in the footer, and in "My stamps". Only people who signed up themselves are on the list. Export it each week and send your email with Brevo or Mailchimp.

    Shown right after they sign up, as a thank-you. Make the code first in Promo codes (tip: 1 use per customer isn't checked by email, so keep it small, like 10% off).

    They see the deal right away, but the code (and Copy / Use buttons) unlock after they enter their email. Signed-up customers see codes straight away. Codes you post yourself (e.g. on Facebook) can still be typed in the cart.

    🍪 Cart suggestions

    "Add something?" in the customer's cart (one tap) and as hints on the POS ticket. Your suggestions come first; then, if switched on, what customers actually buy together; then a popular pastry with a drink, or a popular drink with food.

    Only suggest from

    Picked categories only (e.g. Pastries). None picked = anything. Items named like a promo bundle (e.g. "b1t1", "bundle") are never suggested.

    🎉 Buy 1 Take 1 deals

    Pick the drinks or food in the deal. When a customer (online) or the cashier (POS) has two of them, they're paired automatically: the pair costs the price you set, or the dearer one if you leave the price empty (the other is free). Add-ons are always paid. Promo codes and senior/PWD/staff discounts apply to what's left.
