      Privacy notice

      Demetria's Diner only collects what we need to take your order and run our promos. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

      What we collect and why

      • Your name, order and notes to make your order and call you when it's ready.
      • Delivery address and phone (delivery orders only) so our rider can find you.
      • Mobile number (optional) for your stamp card.
      • Email and first name (optional) only if you sign up for promo emails.
      • Payment reference numbers to confirm GCash / Maya payments. We never see your PIN or card details.

      Who sees it

      Only our staff. Promo emails may be sent through an email service (like Brevo or Mailchimp) on our behalf. We don't sell your information.
